# Experience Thread Draft

Thread on player experience vs coach experience in March. Data: Bart Torvik team tables.

---

## Tweet 1

Image: `reports/figures/experience_context_over_time.png`

To start, I looked at experience over time.

Unsurprisingly, we see a huge jump in average experience since the shift in portal rules.

On the other end, coaching experience has dipped, likely a product of coaches like Roy Williams and Coach K retiring.

---

## Tweet 2

Image: `reports/figures/player_exp_vs_finish_pooled.png`

Next we look at player experience vs. results.

That relationship is basically inverse, as the best teams typically have one-and-dones.

But after controlling for seed, player experience vs. result is effectively neutral.

---

## Tweet 3

Image: `reports/figures/coach_experience_vs_march.png`

The coach side offers a little more insight. The better coaches tend to get further, as expected.

But this trend persists, with a `+0.074` rho, indicating that coaching experience is indeed worth weighting in general.

This is our strongest indicator thus far in the 12 days.

---

## Tweet 4

Image: `reports/figures/combined_experience_archetypes.png`

Here we see great coaches being able to carry the young teams they're used to.

Teams of experienced players can help young coaches to a lesser extent.

While the market seems to catch up to those with all-around experience.

And the data is down on young team + young coach.

---

## Tweet 5

Image: `reports/figures/experience_player_vs_finish_by_seed_group.png`

The player seed-group breakdown is varied.

Player Exp. is a strong indicator for 1-4 seeds and 9-16. While it flips negative for 5-8 seeds.

So it's important to consider seed when weighting Exp.

---

## Tweet 6

Image: `reports/figures/experience_coach_vs_finish_by_seed_group.png`

The coach seed-group breakdown is fairly consistent, with the relevant groups all benefitting from coach experience, aside from the 3-4 seeds.

This is likely not a significant nuance, and coaching experience can be generally understood as a positive come tourney time.

---

## Tweet 7

Image: `reports/figures/experience_by_year.png`

The Year-by-Year view tells the same story, showing Coaching exp. as a positive in `10/12` seasons.

While player exp was positive in only `10` of `17` completed seasons.

---

## Tweet 8

Image: `reports/figures/experience_2026_field.png`

There are many low seeds with strong experience, and some with little, like `Santa Clara` and `Miami OH`, which may draw some concern for them.

There are few top seeds with significant experience so I don't expect it to play a big role towards the finals.

---

## Tweet 9 ? CONCLUSION

Image: `reports/figures/experience_2026_coach_field.png`

Here we see `UConn` and `Houston` with outlier tournament experience, where the coaches may be able to carry their teams further than they otherwise would have.

`Gonzaga`, `Sparty`, and `Kansas` are in the next tier. Expect all these teams to be a tough out.
