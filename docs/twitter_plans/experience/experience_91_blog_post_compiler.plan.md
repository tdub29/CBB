## Required Framing Rules
- Keep the player and coach tracks separate until the combined-archetype section.
- Use raw `Exp.` as the lead player metric throughout the post.
- Treat `exp_rank_season` as supporting context and explain explicitly that lower rank means more experience.
- Make `coach_tourn_games_5y` the lead coaching metric and use `coach_tourn_win_pct_5y` as a sharpening detail.
- Full-sample findings are the base case; recent trends only raise or lower confidence.
