# Visual QC Checklist

## Purpose
This is the mandatory visual review checklist for charts, viewers, and post assets in the `12 Days of Madness` workflow.

Use it before anything is treated as ready for Trevor review.

## Chart Title
- Is there a title at all.
- Is the title large enough to read immediately.
- Does the title state the basketball question or conclusion, not just the metric name.
- Is the title visually prominent instead of buried in the figure.

## Metric Labels
- Are internal stat codes translated into reader-friendly labels where possible.
- If shorthand remains, is it defined early in the thread or viewer.
- Are axis labels explicit about what higher and lower values mean.
- Are units or rate definitions clear enough to avoid ambiguity.

## First-Glance Readability
- Can the reader tell what is being compared in under five seconds.
- Is the key pattern visually obvious without squinting.
- Are fonts large enough for mobile viewing.
- Are important annotations readable.
- Are there too many categories, colors, or lines competing for attention.

## Intuitive Visualization
- Does the chart type fit the question.
- Would a simpler chart make the same point better.
- Is the visual ordering intuitive.
- Are baselines and reference lines used when they help interpretation.
- Are unnecessary gridlines, legends, or decorative elements removed.

## Story Alignment
- Does the chart answer the question the tweet or brief is asking.
- Is the chart doing useful work instead of repeating text.
- Does the visual support the bracket takeaway rather than distract from it.
- If a chart needs a paragraph of explanation to make sense, should it be redesigned.

## Minimum Review Standard
- First review: technical correctness.
- Second review: readability and intuitive understanding.
- Third review if needed: revise titles, labels, annotations, sizing, or chart form.

## Fail Conditions
Do not pass a chart back as ready if any of these are true:
- the title is hard to notice
- the metric labels are confusing
- the chart looks clean but takes too long to understand
- the chart is analytically valid but visually unintuitive
- the writeup is carrying a chart that should have explained itself
