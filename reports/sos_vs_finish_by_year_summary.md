# SOS Analysis 3: SOS vs Finish by Year

- Primary metric: `Elite SOS`
- Measured: each tournament year's Spearman correlation between finish depth and SOS, raw and not seed-adjusted.
- Positive years: `17` of `17`.
- Highest year: `2025` at rho `0.621`.
- Lowest year: `2010` at rho `0.329`.
- Seed drives both SOS and finish depth, so these raw rhos mostly measure seed structure; the seed-centered view is `reports/sos_vs_finish_pooled_summary.md`. No significance test; see `reports/robustness_summary.md`.
