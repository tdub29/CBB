# Robustness Checks: Uncertainty on the Headline Descriptive Claims

Generated by `scripts/robustness_checks.py` (10,000 resamples per test, fixed seeds). Every point
estimate was first re-derived with the same loaders and table builders that wrote the original reports, then
compared with the published file: (b) band values and (c) conference means and medians against the published
CSVs to 6 decimals, (a) against the 3-decimal value printed in
`reports/sos_vs_finish_seed_controlled_summary.md`. The `matches_published` column in each CSV records the result.

## Bottom line

- **(a) SOS within seed line:** rho `+0.061` (n = 1088), 95% CI [-0.001, +0.123], permutation p = 0.041: borderline (permutation and bootstrap disagree at 5%).
  About 52% of the estimate sits at the seed-line x season level: a null that shuffles only among
  the 4 same-seed teams of one season has mean `+0.031`, and against it the association is not

- **(b) Seed-band correlations:** pooled across all 131 cells (the conservative family), 0 survive Benjamini-Hochberg at q < 0.05 (0 at q < 0.10). 9 have nominal p < 0.05, against ~6.6 expected by chance. Smallest pooled q = 0.367 (sos Elite SOS seeds 11-13).
  The count depends on the family.   The published player-experience band values (e.g. `+0.554` for seeds 1-2) come from a

## Method

- **Bootstrap CI (teams), claims (a) and (b):** 95% percentile interval, resampling team-seasons with replacement
  *within their exact seed line* (seed lines keep their size), re-estimating any seed-line centering inside each
  resample.
- **Bootstrap CI (teams), claim (c):** 95% percentile interval from an iid bootstrap of that conference's own
  team-seasons, with each team's seed baseline (its seed line's full-sample mean finish) held fixed. It only sees the
  spread the conference's own sample shows, so when its teams rarely or never beat their seed line it cannot draw
  an upset and comes out too narrow. `boot_sd_ratio` (bootstrap SD / seed-line SD of the mean) and `ci_note`
  flag this: no team above its seed line, or a ratio below 0.75.
- **Bootstrap CI (seasons):** same estimator, resampling whole tournaments. Outcomes inside one bracket are coupled,
  so prefer this interval when it is wider. For conferences a third variant resamples programs. Cluster intervals
  with fewer than 10 clusters are too narrow and are flagged in `ci_note`.
- **Within-seed null band (c):** the 2.5%-97.5% range of the conference mean under the permutation null below, i.e.
  where the mean lands 95% of the time if the conference's teams were random draws from their seed lines. Compare a
  conference's mean with this band, not with 0.
- **Permutation p:** the stat is shuffled *within exact seed line* (outcomes and seeds fixed), so the null keeps all
  seed structure. Two-sided (`2 x` smaller tail, +1 correction). `p_perm_seed_season` shuffles only among the 4 same-seed teams of one season,
  which also holds seed-line x season shifts fixed; it is a narrower, lower-power test.
- **Multiple comparisons:** Benjamini-Hochberg across all 131 seed-band cells in (b) (`q_bh`, primary), and across the 32 conferences in (c). For (b) the CSV also gives BH within each published table
  (`q_bh_within_table`) and within each statistic's bands (`q_bh_within_statistic`).
- **Conference shrinkage:** normal-normal empirical Bayes (DerSimonian-Laird). A conference mean's sampling variance is
  the sum of its teams' seed-line finish variances over n^2, i.e. its variance under the within-seed null.

## Caveats

- These are descriptive checks on 17 tournaments (2008-2025, no 2020); some band cells use a subset (experience coach_exp_rho: 12 seasons, tempo adjt_rho_since_2014: 11 seasons). They ask whether a published number stands
  out from seed-structure noise, not whether it would predict future brackets.
- Percentile bootstrap intervals can under-cover in small, skewed samples (e.g. Horizon: n = 18 with two extreme values). The permutation p-values do not rely on that approximation.
- **Per-conference bootstrap CIs (in the CSV) are unreliable for many conferences.** No team-season beat its seed
  line in SWAC, BSth, BSky, so the bootstrap cannot draw an upset and the interval collapses below zero even where the
  permutation test finds nothing (e.g. SWAC). The bootstrap SD is under 0.75x the seed-line SD in WCC, OVC, AE, Pat, BW, WAC.
  20 of 32 conferences have fewer than 10 programs (Ivy, MVC, SB, MAAC, ASun, Slnd, MAC, NEC, MEAC, WCC, SC, SWAC, CAA, OVC, Sum, AE, Pat, BSth, BSky, WAC), so their program-cluster CI is too narrow. For these rows use `null_lo`/`null_hi`, `p_perm`/`q_bh` and the EB interval.
- In seeds 14-16, 90%-92% of teams share their seed line's most common finish, so a single deep run moves the
  estimate and bootstrap distributions can be bimodal (e.g. experience coach_tourn_games_5y: `+0.208`, CI [-0.320, +0.268]). Trust the permutation p there.
- Cells overlap (tempo since 2014 is a subset of tempo all years; offense/defense metrics share teams), so the
  tests are dependent. BH controls FDR under positive dependence; since nothing survives pooled BH, nothing would survive the stricter Benjamini-Yekutieli correction either.
- The empirical-Bayes interval treats tau^2 as known (plug-in), so it is approximate.
