## Formatting Notes

- **Use "🧵" only on the hook tweet** — not on every tweet
- **Front-load the number** when shock value is the trigger
- **Front-load the question** when curiosity is the trigger
- **Keep hooks under 200 characters** when possible — leaves room for the image preview
- **Never open with "Thread:" or "THREAD"** — the content should make it obvious
- **Avoid "Interesting fact:"** or "Fun stat:" — these are reader-repellent filler phrases
